Local TTS downloads

Book2Audio GO can use local text-to-speech to turn book chapters into audio on your computer. Integration for the models below is being tested.

  • Qwen3-TTS

    A multilingual model with nine preset voices, including Japanese and Chinese voices.

    16 GB RAM · NVIDIA CUDA 6 GB VRAM · SSD 10 GB
    Recommended
    32 GB RAM · NVIDIA CUDA ≥ 8 GB VRAM · SSD 20 GB
    Setup

    Install qwen-tts and CUDA-enabled PyTorch. Download the complete model repository, including speech_tokenizer, then run the official demo.

The runtime and supporting model files are required. Planning estimates for Windows 10/11 x64. Hardware has not been benchmarked.
